Scan safely. Create QR codes. Track what happens next.

QR Code by teag.me is a fast, private QR scanner and creator. Point your camera at a code to see its real destination before opening it, or turn any website into a clean QR code you can save and share.

WHAT IT DOES
- Scan any QR code with your camera, instantly
- See the full link and its domain up front, before you open anything
- Open the link, or copy it to use later
- Create static QR codes free without an account
- Save QR images to Photos or share them anywhere
- Sign in to create editable teag.me codes with scan analytics
- Read Wi-Fi codes and plain text just as easily
- Keeps a private URL history on your device
- Optionally syncs URL-only history when you sign in
- Works the moment you open it. No setup or account required.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
- Camera and photo decoding happen on your device.
- Anonymous scanning requires no account.
- Only URL history is synced when you explicitly sign in.
- Wi-Fi passwords and plain-text codes are never saved or uploaded.
- Privacy-safe product analytics never include scanned content, URLs, photos, credentials, or precise location. No ads or cross-app tracking.

The whole point of a QR code is that you can't read it with your own eyes. QR Code by teag.me gives you back that step—and makes it just as easy to create a code of your own.
